Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026 · Effective: 18 September 2026

Oufy is a wardrobe styling app. You photograph the clothes you own, and Oufy suggests outfits and shows you how they might look on you using AI-generated imagery. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, who processes it on our behalf, and how you delete it.

The short version. We collect what the app needs to work: your account details, your style preferences, the photos you add, and basic technical data about how the app runs. We do not sell your data, we show no ads, and we do not track you across other apps or websites. Deleting your account permanently removes your photos, including any photo of yourself.

1. Who we are

Oufy is operated by Muhammad Shafain Kazi, an individual developer based in Hyderabad, Pakistan, trading as "Oufy" ("we", "us", "our"). We are the data controller for the information described in this policy.

For any privacy question, request or complaint, and for general support, contact support@oufy.site.

2.3 What we do not collect

  • No body measurements. We never ask for your height, weight, clothing size, body shape or skin tone, and we do not derive them.
  • No location data. The app does not request location permission and does not collect your location.
  • We do not access your contacts, calendar, microphone, health data or browsing history.
  • We use no advertising identifiers, we run no ads, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites — which is why the app never shows an App Tracking Transparency prompt.

3. How we use your information

  • To provide the service — cataloguing your wardrobe, generating outfit recommendations, producing virtual try-on images, and syncing your wardrobe across your devices.
  • To personalise what you see — your style profile, your saved outfits and the attributes of your garments feed the recommendation engine.
  • To process purchases — validating your App Store purchase and maintaining your membership status.
  • To keep the service working and secure — diagnostics, crash reporting, abuse prevention and rate limiting.
  • To communicate with you — service messages about your account, your membership or important changes to the app, sent by email to the address associated with your sign-in.

This version of Oufy sends no notifications. The app does not ask for notification permission, does not register a push token, and has no marketing or reminder messages to send you. If we introduce notifications in a later version we will ask your permission first, give you a way to turn them off inside the app, and update this policy before doing so.

We do not use your photos to train general-purpose AI models, and we do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone.

4. Your photos and AI processing

Photos are the most sensitive thing this app handles, so we want to be specific about them.

  • Where they are stored. Your images are held in cloud storage under a location tied to your account, and server-side security rules restrict access to your authenticated account. Generated try-on images are stored privately rather than being made publicly browsable.
  • How they are processed. To remove backgrounds from garment photos, to derive a garment's attributes, and to produce AI-powered virtual try-on images, your images are sent to specialist third-party AI processing providers that we engage for that purpose. For a try-on, this includes both your own photo and the garment images you selected. These providers process the images only to return a result to us; we do not authorise them to use your images for their own purposes or to train their own models.
  • AI-generated images. Try-on results are AI-generated images, not photographs. See our Terms of Service for what that means in practice.
  • Try-on is optional. You choose whether to add a photo of yourself, you can replace it at any time, and you can delete it.
  • Use only your own likeness. Uploading a photo of another identifiable person without their consent breaches our Terms of Service.

We operate from Pakistan, and our infrastructure and processing providers operate in the United States and other countries. Using Oufy therefore means your information is transferred to and processed outside your own country, including in countries whose data protection laws may differ from those where you live.

Where we transfer personal data out of the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we rely on appropriate safeguards for that transfer, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (and the UK Addendum where applicable), or another lawful transfer mechanism.

7. How long we keep it

  • Account, wardrobe and style-profile data — for as long as your account exists.
  • Analytics and crash data — retained on our providers' standard schedules, generally up to 14 months.
  • Purchase and transaction records — retained where necessary for tax, accounting, fraud prevention and other legal obligations. These are kept separately from your account and only for as long as the law requires.
  • After deletion — deleting your account removes your data straight away. We keep no archive or backup from which a deleted account can be restored, and we cannot recover it for you afterwards, however soon you ask. Any residual copies inside our providers' infrastructure are overwritten in the ordinary course, are not accessible through the app, and are never returned to your account.

8. Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account from inside the app: Profile → Delete account — tap your avatar on the Home screen to open Profile. If your membership has ended, or you never started one, the same Delete account action sits on the membership screen, so you never need an active subscription to delete your account. Deletion is immediate and irreversible — there is no recovery period, and signing in again afterwards creates a new, empty account. It permanently removes:

  • your account record, profile and style profile;
  • every wardrobe item, saved outfit, try-on result and setting;
  • every stored image, including original photos, generated cutouts, any photo of yourself, and generated try-on images.

We keep no copy from which the account can be brought back, so neither you nor we can undo this — if you sign in again afterwards you start over with a new, empty account. Deleting your account is not the same as cancelling your subscription — an active subscription belongs to your Apple ID, not to your Oufy account, and must be cancelled separately through Apple or it will continue to bill you after your account is gone. You can also email support@oufy.site to request deletion.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, delete or restrict the processing of your personal information, to object to certain processing, and to withdraw consent where we rely on it. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority. To exercise any right, email support@oufy.site. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

9.1 EEA and UK (GDPR / UK GDPR)

Our legal bases for processing are:

  • Performance of a contract — creating your account, storing and processing your wardrobe, generating recommendations and try-on images, and managing your membership.
  • Legitimate interests — keeping the service secure and reliable, preventing abuse and fraud, diagnosing crashes, and understanding how the app is used so we can improve it. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests at any time.
  • Consent — where you grant the app access to your camera or photo library. You can withdraw this at any time in your device settings, though the app cannot add garments or run try-on without it.
  • Legal obligation — retaining transaction records for tax and accounting purposes.

9.2 California (CCPA / CPRA)

In the twelve months before the date of this policy, we collect the categories of personal information described in section 2: identifiers, customer records, commercial information (purchase history), internet and app activity, visual information (your photos), and inferences drawn to personalise your recommendations. We collect these for the business purposes described in section 3 and disclose them to the service providers described in section 5.1.

We do not sell or share your personal information as the CCPA and CPRA define those terms, and we do not do so for cross-context behavioural advertising. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18, so we do not sell or share the personal information of minors.

10. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest by our cloud provider. Access to stored files is restricted to your authenticated account by server-side security rules, and our backend accepts only attested requests from genuine copies of the app. Access to live user data is restricted to the people who build and operate Oufy, protected by individually credentialed accounts with multi-factor authentication, and used only for support, debugging and investigating abuse. No system is perfectly secure, but we take this seriously — particularly for photos of you.

11. Age requirement

Oufy is intended for people aged 18 or over. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 has provided us with personal information, contact support@oufy.site and we will delete it.
